# Clap Kartel

> Clap Kartel is a premier cinema and entertainment industry networking platform connecting actors, directors, technicians across 24 film crafts with casting calls, equipment rentals, filmmaking lessons, industry updates, talent showcases, and professional collaboration.

## Core Features & Sections

- [24 Crafts](https://clapkartel.com/craft): Comprehensive directory and talent matching across all 24 filmmaking crafts including Direction, Cinematography, Editing, Acting, Screenplay Writing, Music Direction, Choreography, Stunts, Art Direction, and Sound Design.
- [Casting Calls & Auditions](https://clapkartel.com/casting): Verified casting notices, audition announcements, and artist requirements for feature films, web series, short films, ad films, and commercial projects.
- [Cinema Lessons](https://clapkartel.com/lessons): Filmmaking masterclasses, acting workshops, direction tutorials, cinematography techniques, and behind-the-scenes master guides.
- [Rentals & Equipment](https://clapkartel.com/rentals): Film cameras, lenses, lighting rigs, sound gear, studio spaces, shooting floors, and location equipment rentals.
- [Talent Directory](https://clapkartel.com/directory): Searchable directory of verified industry artists, technicians, cast, and crew members with rich portfolio profiles.
- [Actors & Actresses](https://clapkartel.com/cast-crew): Dedicated spotlights and directories for male, female, and child actors, voiceover artists, and background cast.
- [Directors & Crew](https://clapkartel.com/director): Experienced and emerging film directors, assistant directors, creative producers, and cinematographers.
- [Reels & Video Showcases](https://clapkartel.com/reels): Short-form cinema videos, audition tapes, showreels, and performance highlights.
- [Other Sections & Industry Insights](https://clapkartel.com/other-section): Daily posts, music releases, industry news, award updates, trailers, and film festival announcements.

## Community & Networking

- [User Profiles](https://clapkartel.com/user-profile): Complete professional profiles with craft specializations, filmography, media galleries, contact info, and experience records.
- [Direct Messaging](https://clapkartel.com/messages): Real-time communication and collaboration between creators, production houses, and artists.
- [Notifications](https://clapkartel.com/notification): Real-time alerts for casting updates, application statuses, network connections, and community posts.
- [Wishlist & Following](https://clapkartel.com/followers): Bookmarked profiles, favorite artists, and saved listings.

## Authentication & Account

- [Login with OTP](https://clapkartel.com/login): Secure mobile number and SMS OTP verification.
- [Login with Password](https://clapkartel.com/login-password): Direct mobile number and password authentication.
- [Sign Up](https://clapkartel.com/signup): Account creation for artists, technicians, production houses, and cinema fans.
- [Account Settings](https://clapkartel.com/account-settings): Profile management, craft selection, location preferences, and account security.
- [Privacy Policy](https://clapkartel.com/privacy-policy): Privacy commitments, data protection, and user rights.
- [Terms & Conditions](https://clapkartel.com/terms-conditions): Terms of service and community standards.
